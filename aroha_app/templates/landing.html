THE AROHA SYSTEM

Three Dedicated Roles, One Caring Barangay

Designed for Philippine local government communities to help ensure every senior citizen gets the support they need.

FA

ROLE 01 • FAMILIES

For Family Members

Request daily assistance, medical runs, or companionship for your elder relatives. Include their address and monitor real-time updates.

  • Submit requests with detailed address & phone
  • Track status: Pending, In Progress, Completed
  • View request history and assigned volunteers
● PORTAL ROLEFamily Access & Requests
V

ROLE 02 • VOLUNTEERS

For Volunteers

Support seniors right in your neighborhood. Browse open requests filtered by request type, barangay, and status, accept tasks, and coordinate with barangay staff.

  • Browse requests by barangay & category
  • Accept companionship or grocery errands
  • Access senior contact and visit details
● VOLUNTEER WORKFLOWFeed & Neighborhood Triage
BS

ROLE 03 • BARANGAY STAFF

For Barangay Staff

The administrative command center for the barangay. Filter incoming requests by type, date, and sector, assign verified volunteers, and transition statuses.

  • Filter by Request Type, Date, & Barangay
  • Assign registered volunteers to pending tasks
  • Status control: Pending, In Progress, Completed
● ADMINISTRATIVE HUBDispatch Console & Records
